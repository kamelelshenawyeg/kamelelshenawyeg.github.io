Fresh Freeze

IndustryFrozen food
MarketEgypt
ProjectPackage design - social media campaign

Fresh Freeze, a pioneer in frozen food solutions in Egypt, introduced innovative products such as frozen minced garlic, ginger, and garlic with coriander. The challenge was that customers struggled to distinguish between garlic product variants. To solve this, I redesigned the packaging and launched an awareness campaign across distribution channels, educating customers and enhancing product recognition.

Packaging

I solved Fresh Freeze's packaging challenge by redesigning the packs with clear differentiation for each garlic variant. The new design, combined with an awareness campaign, educated customers and improved recognition across all distribution channels.

View image ↗

Social media

I launched a social media awareness campaign to introduce Fresh Freeze's new packaging, highlighting the differences between product variants through engaging content and educational posts. Customers could easily identify their preferred flavor while trust in the brand grew.
