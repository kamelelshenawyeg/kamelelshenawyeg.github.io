Case study

Fresh Freeze

Clearer product recognition, from redesigned packs to customer education.

Pack redesignPackaging
Awareness rolloutCampaign rollout
Qualitative resultReported result

Documented timing
The packaging-development period, campaign dates and measurement window are not supplied.

Fresh Freeze's packaging redesign and awareness campaign helped customers distinguish its garlic variants and identify their preferred flavour. The changes ran from the pack itself to communication across distribution channels, giving the product range a clearer, consistent presentation.

01

Solve the choice problem on the pack itself

Customers were struggling to distinguish Fresh Freeze's garlic variants. The range included frozen minced garlic, ginger and garlic with coriander. Because the confusion concerned the products themselves, the work began with packaging rather than asking a social campaign to explain an unclear pack range.

02

Make differences visible within one recognisable range

The redesigned packs retained a shared Fresh Freeze identity while giving variants distinct colour and ingredient cues. The original packaging artwork shows those differences carried through a consistent layout. The design addressed two tasks together: help shoppers identify the variant and keep the products recognisable as one brand.

View image ↗

03

Explain the redesign beyond the shelf

An awareness campaign introduced the new packaging across distribution channels. Social and educational posts explained flavour differences and repeated the new pack designs, with practical-use messages alongside product introductions. The campaign therefore carried forward the same recognition problem the packaging had been designed to address.

04

Carry product recognition through the whole campaign

The result was a differentiated product range supported by communication that explained the choice. The packaging and social materials used the same variant cues, so customers could encounter a consistent product identity across the pack and the campaign. The project account reports improved recognition and easier identification of the preferred flavour.
