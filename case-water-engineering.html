Water Engineering Consultations

IndustryEngineering consultation - infrastructure engineering & water treatment
Target marketsEgypt, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Libya
Initial presence1 office in Egypt
Current presence4 branches across the Middle East

Water Engineering Consultations brought me in at an early stage, when the firm ran a single office in Egypt. I built a structured, data-driven marketing framework that supported the firm's regional expansion, ending with four branches across the Middle East.

Strategic foundation

The engagement commenced with a comprehensive B2B market analysis of the engineering consultation sector in Egypt, with a focus on water infrastructure and treatment projects. This was followed by an assessment of regional opportunities in Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Libya. The objective was to identify key competitors achieving measurable growth and to benchmark their strategies.

  • Based on these insights, a targeted marketing framework: manpower plan
  • Action plan
  • Budget plan

  • Industry events & community engagement
  • Geographically focused advertising
  • Data acquisition & qualification
  • LinkedIn campaigns
  • SMS campaigns
  • Email campaigns

Industry events & community engagement

  • Conducted behavioral analysis of the target sectors and audiences in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Libya
  • Identified scientific conferences as a strategic entry point for engagement
  • Reframed selected conference topics in alignment with community marketing principles
  • Leveraged the expertise of the company's consultants and leading engineering professors to reinforce credibility
  • Established professional connections through the public relations department, ensuring structured communication channels and fostering new partnerships

Data acquisition & qualification

  • Extracted and validated contact data for consultants and contractors in each of the four markets
  • Applied rigorous qualification criteria, ensuring accuracy and relevance of contact details
  • Direct accessibility to decision-makers rather than intermediaries
  • Verified participation in national water treatment tenders

Implementation timeline

  • Month 1: development and activation of all communication channels
  • Months 2-3: development and activation of all communication channels; allocation of resources toward the most effective channels
  • Month 3 post: introduction of sequential content strategies for underperforming channels, followed by additional evaluation after three months

Outcomes & achievements

Within one year of implementation, Water Engineering Consultations realized:

  • Geographic expansion with new offices established in Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Libya, in addition to Egypt
  • Accelerated project turnover and improved profitability in international markets, primarily supported by email campaigns
  • Strengthened brand equity and recognition within the regional scientific and engineering community
