Case file 01

 

Water Engineering Consultations

A B2B market-entry programme across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Libya.1

  • Four markets
  • One year
  • B2B, not consumer

The framework2

A growth loop marketing framework.

The programme ran on it from the start. Six stages, each one feeding the next; the results return to the market and start the next turn. The exhibits below are those stages, in the order the work ran.

  1. 01The market
  2. 02Who to talk to
  3. 03How outreach ran
  4. 04The email channel
  5. 05The events and conferences
  6. 06What it produced

Exhibit 01The market

Expansion was a market-entry problem, not an advertising one.

Egypt first, then Saudi Arabia, Oman and Libya, benchmarked against competitors before any manpower, action or budget plan was written.

Exhibit 02Who to talk to

Qualified before anything was sent.

  • Participation in national water-treatment tenders

The record

One database, split into consultants and contractors, each with its own sequence and value proposition.

checked before sending

Note 4

Exhibit 03How outreach ran

A dedicated team, not a mail merge.

LinkedIn campaigns broadened reach across the four markets, backed by the company’s project library and personalised outreach. The public relations manager reported monthly on relationship-building outside Egypt, so campaign activity stayed connected to ongoing professional contact rather than to ad delivery alone.

  1. Two sources sit behind this page: the year-one outcome (four offices, quarterly growth above 600%) and the advertising/project report (106 leads, 13 projects, revenue above EGP 91M). The source does not assign all of that revenue to email.
  2. The framework is named by the person who led the work: a growth loop in which the results feed back into the market. The six stages shown are the page’s own exhibits, in their order; no stage name or figure is added.
  3. Markets assessed: Egypt first, then Saudi Arabia, Oman and Libya, with competitor benchmarking before the manpower, action and budget plans were written.

  6. The email report is the source for these three figures. The project account identifies email as a main contributor to improved international project turnover and profitability.
  7. Conference topics were shaped with the company’s consultants and engineering professors, while public relations maintained professional connections.
  8. Documented timing: the outcome horizon is the first year of implementation. Individual advertising and email report dates are not supplied.
