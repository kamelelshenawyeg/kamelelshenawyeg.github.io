Brand identity

OmegaWater Technology

SectorWater & wastewater treatment
BaseEl-Salheya El-Gedida, Egypt
MarketsEgypt, the Gulf, Africa
ScopeIdentity system & launch

Omega Water Technology is Egypt's first local MBBR manufacturer, designing and building integrated water and wastewater treatment solutions: carriers, aeration systems, screens and full treatment plants, delivered to municipal and industrial clients.

01 · The mark

A rising system

The logo pairs a fan of tapered rays with a split horizon line above the wordmark. Every lockup used across the brand comes from one construction.

Our reading
Rays over a horizon. The fan reads as a rising sun and as flow radiating from a single source, set in the hues of moving water. The split baseline under the mark keeps the composition grounded, like a waterline. This is our interpretation of the design intent, not a quote from the brand.
