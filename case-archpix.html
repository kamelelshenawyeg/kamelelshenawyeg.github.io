Archpix Engineering Consultants

CompanyArchpix Engineering Consultants
LocationDubai & Abu Dhabi
Target audienceUAE corporate entities; major investors in Dubai and Abu Dhabi
IndustryLuxury homes design & build - engineering consultations

Archpix Engineering Consultants came to me with expensive ads, weak conversion, and an unclear brand. I rebuilt the identity, launched a signature campaign, shipped a professional online platform, and automated the marketing workflow. Archpix now runs a scalable, high-value marketing system that reflects its prestige in constructing palaces and luxury homes across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Key challenges

  • High advertising costs in reaching targeted clients through sponsored ads
  • Weak lead conversion, as clients were not following up during the deal-closing stage
  • Limited acquisition channels, with new projects coming mainly through referrals and personal networks
  • Unclear brand identity, attracting inquiries from clients below the firm's service scope
  • Single-channel dependency, relying solely on Instagram for publishing content
  • Outdated website with poor user experience and no potential for development

Action plan

  • Discovery & research phase: full macro & micro environmental analysis
  • Defined the marketing message and a slogan to carry the brand's UVP
  • Developed a marketing plan with budget, manpower, and timeline to execute the action plan
  • Brand identity rebuild: designed a complete visual identity ensuring selling points were clearly communicated
  • Introduced company services via manager-led video presentations
  • Developed a structured social media plan and a professional system for showcasing projects

Signature campaign - new brand identity

  • Produced prelaunch campaign; managed end-to-end production: scriptwriting, location scouting, casting, and filming
  • Production was carried out in Egypt to reduce costs
  • Produced VFX-based visual content to follow up the launch campaign
  • Successfully launched promotional campaigns announcing the new identity

Remote content management

  • Designed a structured project library divided into sections and filters
  • Received raw production material and carried out professional post-production editing
  • Developed content production files with technical guidelines
  • Projects library structure: synchronize content creation with operation sheet and CRM
  • Showcase projects by tender stage or construction, etc.

Online platform

  • Built a professional online platform tailored to the new visual identity
  • Optimized the platform for search engine visibility to increase reach
  • Added a project portfolio with advanced filtering features
  • Launched a blog for publishing industry-related articles
