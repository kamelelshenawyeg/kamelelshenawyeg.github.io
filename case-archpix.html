Case study

Archpix Engineering Consultants

230% growth in inbound project inquiries, reaching 150 per month.

For Archpix Engineering Consultants, the result was a larger inbound pipeline and a 100% improvement in advertising ROAS before the testing phase ended. Achieving that required more than increasing media spend. The work rebuilt the premium brand presentation, organised how projects were documented and published, and expanded acquisition beyond Instagram and referrals.

01

Fix the mismatch before paying for more attention

Archpix designs and builds luxury homes and palaces in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. It faced high advertising costs, weak follow-up at the deal-closing stage and inquiries below its service scope. An unclear identity and an outdated website compounded the problem. I began with macro- and micro-environment analysis, defined the marketing message and value proposition, and built an execution plan covering budget, people and timing.

02

Reintroduce the firm as a premium construction brand

The identity rebuild clarified the firm's selling points and supported a launch campaign produced with the production team. The work covered scriptwriting, location scouting, casting and filming, with production in Egypt to reduce costs. Follow-up VFX material, promotional activity and manager-led service videos extended the launch into an ongoing explanation of the business.

03

Turn project documentation into a repeatable content operation

A structured project library organised material by sections, filters and tender or construction stage. Raw footage moved into post-production through technical guidelines, while content creation was coordinated with the operation sheet and CRM. The purpose was practical: make the firm's existing work available for consistent presentation, instead of depending on isolated posts or whoever happened to have the latest project files.

04

Build an online platform that the operation could maintain

The website translated the new identity into a filterable portfolio and an industry blog, supported by search optimisation. An automation workflow transferred project data and articles from Google Sheets to the platform. This connected publishing to the project-content operation and reduced dependence on manual updates for every item.

View image ↗

05

Test acquisition, then scale the successful advertising

An allocated budget was used to test campaigns across TikTok, Snapchat and Instagram. A media-buying plan then scaled successful ads, with optimisation focused on cost per actual client acquisition. The resulting record shows 230% growth in inbound inquiries, a monthly volume of 150 and a 100% increase in ROAS before testing was complete.
