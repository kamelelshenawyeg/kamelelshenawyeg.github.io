PeakMarketing agency

SectorMarketing & advertising
BaseEgypt
ValuesFusion · Dynamic · Honor
ScopeThe agency's own identity

Numbers talk!

The agency's published brand line is "It's Always Matter of Numbers." Every layout in the system starts from that promise: dark stages, white evidence type, and one signal color that points at the result.

01 · The mark

B for Peak

One monogram carries the whole system: it closes every post, signs every template, and scales from a profile avatar to an oversized outline graphic.

The brand's own story
A letter B drawn as two upward arrows. The monogram is the letter B built from two arrows pointing up, the agency's shorthand for growth, and it reads as "B for Peak". This is the identity owner's own account of the design, not our interpretation.

02 · Color

Ink, white, one signal

The system runs on near-black stages and white type, cut by a single hot signal color. The deeper ember tone appears only as ambient glow at the edges of layouts. Values sampled from the brand's published artwork.

03 · Typography

Loud evidence, quiet captions

Headlines are set in a heavy geometric sans, uppercase and tight; supporting text drops to a light neo-grotesque with generous spacing. No editable type specimens exist in the documented material, so the letterforms are shown inside the brand's own published artwork rather than named or redrawn.

Display · heavy geometric

Uppercase, tight tracking, maximum weight: headlines carry the evidence. Shown in the brand's artwork; this is our reading of the style, not a font identification.

Statement · boxed sans

Brand statements set in wide-tracked light caps, often locked inside a signal-color bar with endpoint markers.

04 · Stationery

Carried by the team

Business card and letterhead fronts, designed for this study in the brand's own system: ink ground, white evidence type, signal accents, the mark as signature. Per the agency's instruction, the stationery carries Kamel Elshenawy's name as a Peak employee.

Our reading
Flat artwork, not a photo mockup. These are new designs drawn in the brand's system for this page. They are not scans of printed agency stationery, and no photoreal render is faked.

05 · In use

The template machine

The identity runs as a publishing system: a category kicker, the agency handle and year up top, evidence in the middle, the mark closing every frame, and the monogram reused as an oversized outline graphic. Posts from the agency's own feed.

Scope notes

  • Documented on this page: the monogram and its usage, the working palette, the letterforms inside published artwork, new stationery designs in the brand's system, and the social template family.
  • Colors are sampled pixel values from the agency's published posts; names like Signal and Ember are descriptive, not official brand names.
  • The monogram artwork is cleaned from the agency's own published posts; no original vector file exists in the documented material, so the artwork is presented at sizes where it stays crisp.
  • The stationery designs are new flat artwork created for this page in the brand's style, carrying Kamel Elshenawy's name as a Peak employee per the agency's instruction; they are not presented as existing printed pieces.
  • The brand line "It's Always Matter of Numbers." is quoted exactly as the agency publishes it.
