Case study

Heliopolis Coffee

211,684 engagements for a new breakfast launch.

211,684Campaign engagements
Breakfast launchWork delivered

Documented timing
The campaign reporting period and production dates are not supplied in the original project material.

Heliopolis Coffee introduced its thyme chicken breakfast through a campaign that kept the brand's refined coffee-store character intact. A branch photo shoot supplied the launch visuals and social posts, generating 211,684 recorded engagements around the new breakfast offer.

01

Give the new product a place in the brand

The brief was to introduce a new breakfast item to a wider audience without making it feel disconnected from Heliopolis Coffee. The campaign used breakfast as a cheerful daily ritual, connecting the product launch to the atmosphere and experience of the existing brand.

02

Build the launch around the branch and the food

The photo shoot took place at the Heliopolis branch. Product-focused views of the thyme chicken breakfast were combined with the coffee-store setting, giving the new item both appetite appeal and a familiar brand context. Those images became the campaign's visual material rather than introducing an unrelated graphic style.

View image ↗

03

Carry the same launch idea into social content

The social executions kept the new breakfast item, the branch setting and the brand's visual character together. The campaign recorded 211,684 engagements. Its creative approach gave the new item a recognisable place in the Heliopolis Coffee experience, rather than treating it as a stand-alone menu announcement.
