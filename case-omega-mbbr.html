Case study 05

Omega MBBR

IndustryManufacturing
MarketEgypt & Gulf region
ProjectMedia coverage

Omega MBBR manufactures advanced plastic media that expand the surface area of water treatment tanks, growing the beneficial bacteria that purify water. The challenge was perception: the market assumed this engineering-driven technology only came from East Asia or America, while the Middle East needed it urgently for infrastructure. My mission was to build awareness across public, governmental, and commercial sectors about the product's quality and its pioneering role as the first of its kind manufactured in the region, and to position the brand as a trusted innovator in water treatment.

Event organization - Watrex Expo

  • Supported the company in organizing its first major appearance at the Water Expo in Egypt, creating a strong market entry
  • Secured comprehensive media and press coverage, positioning the brand as the most recognized name at the event even before its launch
  • Executed targeted advertising and promotional campaigns that drove demand to the extent that both production lines operated at full capacity for an entire year
